# OBEY? LOL. — Release notes

## Version 2.1.0

**Ruin everything. On an iPad.** Bossworth now has a nemesis (you), a sidekick who betrays him
(U.N.D.O.), and a cousin he made a bet with (Clive).

**New**
- **Ruin it:** many levels can now be finished in a deliberately terrible way — press the button until
  it breaks, punch through the fake wall, agree to the terms four times. It counts. Bossworth melts down.
  Collect all 16 in your **Sabotage file**.
- **U.N.D.O.:** a glitchy sidekick program that leaks Bossworth's chats and tips you off on how to
  sabotage him.
- **Sabotage Studio:** 4 bonus microgames after Level 12 — buttons labelled with the wrong colours, a
  phone that never quite reaches 100%, a tower you can cheat, and a tree that wants to stand on its tip.
- **A narrator who notices:** he reacts when you go quiet, interrupt him or poke him; takes phone calls;
  remembers what you did; loses his composure; writes performance reviews; and fires you (sort of).
- **Fairer help:** a nudge when you're stuck, a highlight on the final hint, "The trick" explained after
  every level, and **Skip** for the levels you want to come back to.
- **Daily Dare** with streaks and badges, **share cards** with no spoilers, personal bests, a speedrun
  timer and hidden **jigsaw pieces**.
- **Made for iPad:** every iPad in both orientations, Split View, Add to Home Screen, offline play,
  keyboard shortcuts, no accidental zoom or long-press menus.
- **Accessibility:** high contrast, extra-large text, plain font, left-handed layout, hold assist, sound
  captions, separate volumes and optional music.
- **Up to 3 players** on one device, and **save codes** to move your progress anywhere.
- **Tested hard:** an independent review found 19 problems before release; all are fixed. Tested in
  Chrome and in Safari's engine on every update.

**How to update:** reload the game. Your progress carries over automatically.

## Version 2.0.0

**Bigger, louder, sillier.** The campaign more than doubles and Bossworth finally has a voice.

**New**
- **20 new levels** (36 in total, in 9 chapters): a volume knob that hides a whisper, Opposite Day,
  a cat sleeping on a lost sock, Bossworth Says, a shy button, words that weigh their letters,
  a narrator who naps at three, wet paint, zero gravity, a copycat cat, a mirror that tells the
  truth, Bossworth's twin Kevin, a leaking pipe, swamp stones, a secret TV channel, a very literal
  password, a card trick, lights out, terms and conditions, and a pop quiz before the finale.
- **Difficulty:** Easy, Medium or Hard. Pick when you start; change any time.
- **Bossworth talks:** expressive babble (or your device's speech), with a moving mouth.
- **Funny wrong answers:** pies, rubber chickens, sad trombones, zaps and splashes, then a card that
  tells you how to do it right.
- **Show me how:** a guided, step-by-step walkthrough of any level.
- **Trophy room:** chapter cups in Bronze, Silver and Gold, plus five special trophies.
- **Better sound effects** throughout.

**Your 1.x save:** kept automatically. Your old finale (Levels 15–16) becomes Levels 35–36, and you
continue from Level 15, the first new level. Endings, secrets, achievements and cosmetics are kept.

**Compatibility:** verified in Chromium (desktop and mobile emulation). Firefox, Safari, real phones
and screen readers are not yet verified — see `TEST-REPORT.md`.
